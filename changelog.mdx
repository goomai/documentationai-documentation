## What changed
- Introduced reusable snippets: store `.mdx`, `.md`, or `.jsx` files under `snippets/` and import them into any page.
- Snippets deploy automatically — no navigation entry required.
- Snippet content is inlined into importing pages for search indexing; snippets are not indexed as standalone pages.
- JSX snippets are searchable via their exported `description` string.
- Changing or deleting a snippet automatically refreshes all pages that import it.
