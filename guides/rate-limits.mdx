# Rate limits

To keep the API fast and reliable for everyone, requests are limited based on your plan tier.

## Limits by plan

| Plan | Requests per minute |
|------|----------------------|
| Free | 60 |
| Pro | 600 |
| Enterprise | Custom |

Need a higher limit? Enterprise plans can request a custom rate limit tailored to your usage.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Exceeding your plan's rate limit returns an HTTP 429 response. Back off and retry after the time indicated in the response before sending further requests.
</Callout>
