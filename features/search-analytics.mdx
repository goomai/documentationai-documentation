## Overview

Search Analytics shows what your readers search for and how well your documentation serves those queries. Use these insights to find content gaps, understand reader behavior, and prioritize improvements.

## Available metrics

- **Search volume:** View charts showing how many searches readers perform over time.
- **Result performance:** Review click-through rates, no-result queries, and the pages that perform best in search results.
- **Search events:** Inspect individual search events in a table, including queries, timestamps, and result clicks.

## Accessing the analytics dashboard

Navigate to **Dashboard > Analytics > Search**. The dashboard loads your search volume, result performance, and search event data.

<Callout kind="info">
Search Analytics is available to site owners and admins. Readers and editors may not have access.
</Callout>

## Privacy and debouncing

Search events are debounced and validated before they are recorded. Debouncing prevents repeated searches from creating duplicate events while readers are still typing or refining a query.

Stored data is aggregated where possible to limit the amount of individual reader activity retained in analytics.

<Callout kind="alert">
Search Analytics must be enabled in your site settings. It is not enabled by default for all plans.
</Callout>
